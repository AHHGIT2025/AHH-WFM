# Workflow Matrix

This matrix defines the standard statuses, triggers, and authorized transitioning roles for each phase of the commercial deal lifecycle.

## 1. CRM & Enquiries
*   **DRAFT**: Initial enquiry draft (Sales Exec).
*   **ACTIVE**: Enquiry submitted for qualification (Sales Exec).
*   **QUALIFIED**: Lead verified and confirmed viable (Sales Lead).
*   **CONVERTED**: Opportunity created; Enquiry closed (System).
*   **REJECTED**: Lead flagged as unqualified or duplicate (Sales Lead).

## 2. Opportunities
*   **PROSPECTING**: Initial deal identification.
*   **SURVEY_DISPATCHED**: Operations surveyor dispatched.
*   **COSTING_INPUT**: Estimator modeling cost cards.
*   **PROPOSAL_SENT**: Quotation submitted to client.
*   **NEGOTIATION**: Client reviewing and negotiating terms.
*   **WON**: Transitioned to contract creation phase.
*   **LOST**: Opportunity closed.

## 3. Pre-Contract Site Surveys
*   **PENDING_DISPATCH**: Scheduled but surveyor not assigned.
*   **DISPATCHED**: Surveyor en route or on-site.
*   **CONDUCTED**: Survey forms filled out and evidence uploaded.
*   **VERIFIED**: Operations Manager verified and approved for Costing.

## 4. Costing & Cost Configurations
*   **DRAFT**: Initial costing calculations.
*   **UNDER_REVIEW**: Submitted for audit checks.
*   **VERIFIED**: Costing verified margin compliance (Finance Lead).
*   **EXCEPTIONAL_APPROVAL**: Approved with custom margin exception (Finance Director).

## 5. Client Quotations
*   **DRAFT**: Proposal document compilation.
*   **SUBMITTED**: Sent to client.
*   **SUPERSEDED**: New version generated; old version locked.
*   **ACCEPTED**: Approved by client.

## 6. Contracts
*   **DRAFT**: Contract setup under review.
*   **APPROVED**: Workflow approved by Legal & Finance.
*   **ACTIVE**: Activated and operational.
*   **TERMINATED**: Closed or deactivated.
