# Screen Inventory (32 Screens)

This document catalogs the 32 screens designed to support the unified Commercial & Contracts lifecycle.

## Phase 1: CRM, Enquiries & Opportunities (Screens 1-8)
1.  **Commercial Dashboard Shell** (Desktop) — `/commercial/dashboard`: KPI metrics and work queues.
2.  **Enquiry Intake Form** (Desktop) — `/commercial/crm/new`: Intake of prospective customer requests.
3.  **Enquiry List Ledger** (Desktop) — `/commercial/crm`: Filtering of lead cases.
4.  **Enquiry Details Page** (Desktop) — `/commercial/crm/[id]`: Status updates and qualification logs.
5.  **Opportunity Kanban Pipeline** (Desktop) — `/commercial/opportunities`: Pipeline stages.
6.  **Opportunity List Ledger** (Desktop) — `/commercial/opportunities/list`: Tabular deal statistics.
7.  **Opportunity Detail Workspace** (Desktop) — `/commercial/opportunities/[id]`: Owner and deal estimates.
8.  **Prospect Client Profiles** (Desktop) — `/commercial/crm/prospects`: Company registration details.

## Phase 2: Site Surveys & Auditing (Screens 9-16)
9.  **Site Survey Dispatch** (Desktop) — `/commercial/surveys/dispatch`: Scheduling surveyor appointments.
10. **Survey Job Calendar** (Desktop) — `/commercial/surveys/calendar`: Dispatch scheduler view.
11. **Survey Field Form** (Desktop) — `/commercial/surveys/new`: Logging site conditions.
12. **Survey Results Workspace** (Desktop) — `/commercial/surveys/[id]`: Technical checklist verification.
13. **Risk & Hazard Registry** (Desktop) — `/commercial/surveys/[id]/risks`: Site hazards and photos.
14. **Template Configurator** (Desktop) — `/settings/pre-contract-config/templates`: Survey form templates.
15. **Survey Manager Dashboard** (Desktop) — `/commercial/surveys/dashboard`: Open surveyor audits.
16. **Site Conditions Audit** (Desktop) — `/commercial/surveys/[id]/conditions`: Tonal layout comparison panel.

## Phase 3: Transactional Costing (Screens 17-24)
17. **Cost Elements Master** (Desktop) — `/settings/pre-contract-config/elements`: Formula settings.
18. **Rate Cards Ledger** (Desktop) — `/settings/pre-contract-config/rates`: Standard rate books.
19. **Cost Package Builder** (Desktop) — `/commercial/costing/new`: Cost estimate modeling workspace.
20. **Margin Calibration Matrix** (Desktop) — `/commercial/costing/[id]/margins`: Profit vs cost element delta.
21. **Reliever Calculations Tab** (Desktop) — `/commercial/costing/[id]/relievers`: Rest day allowance ratios.
22. **Formula Debugger Console** (Desktop) — `/settings/pre-contract-config/debugger`: Live validation check.
23. **Finance Verification Panel** (Desktop) — `/commercial/costing/[id]/audit`: Audit logs.
24. **Cost Package Detail Workspace** (Desktop) — `/commercial/costing/[id]`: Commercial estimates.

## Phase 4: Quotations & Legal Contracts (Screens 25-32)
25. **Quotation Compiler Workspace** (Desktop) — `/commercial/quotations/new`: Proposal builder.
26. **Quotation Version Ledger** (Desktop) — `/commercial/quotations/[id]/versions`: Side-by-side diff.
27. **Quotation Detail Page** (Desktop) — `/commercial/quotations/[id]`: Client proposal terms.
28. **Contract Mobilisation Checklist** (Desktop) — `/commercial/handover`: Cross-departmental tasks.
29. **Addendum Modification Form** (Desktop) — `/manpower/[business]/contracts/[id]/addendum`: Contract amendment.
30. **Unified Activity Timeline** (Desktop) — `/commercial/dashboard/activities`: Chronological deal flow.
31. **Contract Redirect Router** (Desktop) — `/commercial/contracts`: Access-denied/router.
32. **Unified Version comparison** (Desktop) — `/commercial/dashboard/versions`: Side-by-side diff.
